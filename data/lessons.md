# Lessons playbook (updated 2026-11-21 by Gemini)

- When high-impact economic events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) are scheduled within 12 hours -> DO NOT enter new positions; wait for post-event volatility to clear and technical retests to complete.
- When 4H RSI is oversold (< 35) and price is within 20 points of major Daily/4H support -> DO NOT open short trades directly into support; wait for a confirmed candle body close breakdown below support followed by a pullback retest.
- When Daily, 4H, and 1H trends align downward but price is extended far below 1H EMA20 -> WAIT for a pullback retest of 1H EMA20 or 1H EMA50 resistance before executing short positions.
- When Daily and 4H macro trends are DOWN -> DO NOT take counter-trend long trades based solely on oversold RSI conditions without a confirmed 1H market structure shift (higher high and higher low pattern above 1H EMA20).
- When short-term momentum indicators (1H MACD histogram) tick upward while price tests major support -> DO NOT short at market; await lower-high structural rejection at dynamic EMA resistance.
- When valid trade setups offer a risk-to-reward ratio below 1.5:1 to nearest structural support/resistance -> DO NOT take the trade; wait for price location to improve.
- When system encounters stale data (>1 hour old) or multiple AI model errors -> DEFAULT to HOLD and preserve capital.
- When 4H and 1H timeframes display mixed trend signals or price is trapped between key EMAs -> HOLD until multi-timeframe alignment clarifies direction.

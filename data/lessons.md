# Lessons playbook (updated 2026-11-13 by Gemini)

- When 4H and 1H timeframes display mixed trend conditions or price is trapped between key EMAs -> hold and protect capital until trend alignment clarifies across timeframes.
- When price drops aggressively into oversold territory (1H RSI < 35) near recent support during a downtrend -> do not enter short positions late; wait for a pullback to key resistance or EMA levels.
- When Daily, 4H, and 1H timeframes align in a strong downtrend but price is extended far below 1H EMA20 -> hold off on shorting until price retests 1H EMA20/EMA50 resistance.
- When price is in a strong Daily downtrend -> strictly avoid taking counter-trend long trades based purely on oversold RSI readings without a confirmed bullish reversal structure.
- When major economic events (e.g., PPI, CPI, NFP) are imminent while price is extended -> refrain from opening new positions until event volatility clears and price respects defined key levels.

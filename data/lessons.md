# Lessons playbook (updated 2026-11-14 by Gemini)

- When 4H and 1H timeframes display mixed trend signals or price is trapped between key EMAs -> HOLD and preserve capital until timeframe alignment clarifies.
- When price drops rapidly into oversold territory (1H RSI < 35) near key support during a downtrend -> DO NOT open late short positions; wait for a pullback to 1H EMA20/EMA50 resistance.
- When Daily, 4H, and 1H trends align downward but price is extended far below 1H EMA20 -> WAIT for a pullback retest of 1H EMA20 or EMA50 before entering short.
- When price is in a macro Daily downtrend -> AVOID counter-trend long trades based solely on oversold RSI without a confirmed market structure shift and higher-low pattern.
- When high-impact economic events (e.g., PPI, CPI, NFP, FOMC) are scheduled within 6 hours -> DO NOT enter new positions; wait for event volatility to clear and key technical levels to be tested.

- When Daily and 4H trends align -> trade exclusively in that direction as the primary high-probability trend edge.
- When Daily and 4H are bearish and price pulls back to 1H EMA20/EMA50 -> SELL when a bearish rejection candle closes near EMAs, with SL placed above the pullback high.
- When Daily and 4H are bullish and price pulls back to 1H EMA20/EMA50 -> BUY when a bullish rejection candle closes near EMAs, with SL placed below the pullback low.
- When a key structure level breaks (BOS) with a strong 1H close in trend direction -> enter in trend direction on a shallow retest of the broken level.
- When 4H structure forms a fresh CHoCH and 15M pulls back to local demand/EMA support -> BUY or SELL in the direction of the 4H momentum if target to supply/demand allows R:R >= 1.5.
- When 15M micro-structure breaks out of a tight consolidation range in alignment with 1H trend -> enter in breakout direction on 15M candle close.
- Oversold or overbought RSI within an established 1H/4H impulse is normal -> do not skip valid trend retests solely due to RSI readings.
- Do not chase entries far extended from 1H EMAs -> wait for price to pull back near 1H/15M EMAs before entering.
- Do not enter trades directly into unbroken opposing HTF supply/demand zones if distance provides less than 1.5:1 reward-to-risk.
- When price is trapped mid-range between opposing 1H supply and demand zones -> HOLD until price retests a key structural level.

# Lessons playbook (updated 2026-11-12 by Gemini)
<!-- all-time: {"trades": 0} -->

- When 4H and 1H timeframes display mixed trend conditions or price is trapped between key EMAs -> hold and protect capital until trend alignment clarifies across timeframes.
- When price drops aggressively into oversold territory (1H RSI < 35) near recent support during a downtrend -> do not open short positions late; wait for a pullback to key resistance or EMA levels.
- When Daily, 4H, and 1H timeframes all align in a strong downtrend but price is extended away from 1H EMA20 -> hold off on shorting until price retests 1H EMA20/EMA50 resistance.
- When price is in a strong Daily downtrend -> avoid taking counter-trend long trades based purely on oversold RSI readings without a clear reversal structure or bullish divergence.
